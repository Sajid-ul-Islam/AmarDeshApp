### Key Features Identified

1. **News Feed Layout** - Grid-based with hero article + sidebar articles
2. **Category Navigation** - Horizontal scrollable category tabs
3. **Prayer Times Widget** - Shows Fajr, Dhuhr, Asr, Maghrib, Isha times
4. **Most Read Section** - Trending/popular articles list
5. **Video Section** - Embedded video content
6. **Area-wise News** - Division/District-based news filtering
7. **e-Paper Integration** - Link to digital newspaper edition
8. **Image-heavy** - Thumbnails for each article
9. **Time stamps** - Relative time display (e.g., "২ ঘণ্টা আগে")
10. **Responsive Design** - Mobile-first approach

### Technical Observations
- Uses WebP image format for optimization
- Image CDN: images.dailyamardesh.com
- URL pattern: /category/article-id
- Article IDs follow pattern: amd + alphanumeric string
- Bengali numerals used throughout
- RTL not required (Bengali is LTR)

### Mobile App Requirements
- Native-like experience on Android & iOS
- Push notifications for breaking news
- Offline reading capability
- Category-based browsing
- Search functionality
- Share articles
- Dark/Light mode
- Font size adjustment for Bengali text
- Prayer times integration
- Bookmark/Save articles
