## 1. The Core Reality: Why Amar Desh Needs a Mobile App Today

Amar Desh is the symbol of courageous, uncompromising journalism (*"স্বাধীনতার কথা বলে"*). Following the historic July 2024 uprising and the paper’s revival, reader trust in Mahmudur Rahman and Amar Desh is at an all-time peak. 

However, Amar Desh currently has **NO official mobile app** on the Google Play Store or Apple App Store. Here is why that needs to change immediately:

1. **92% of Readers are on Smartphones:**  
   People no longer wait for physical newspapers at roadside stands, nor do they manually type web URLs into mobile browsers. News is read through lock-screen alerts and mobile apps.
2. **Bypass Big Tech Censorship & Algorithms:**  
   Relying on Facebook or Google means foreign algorithms decide who sees your reports. They frequently throttle political journalism and keep 80%+ of advertising revenues. A native mobile app gives Amar Desh a direct, uncensorable broadcast pipeline straight into 1,000,000+ reader pockets.
3. **Reaching the Youth (Gen-Z):**  
   The university students and youth who led the July revolution admire Amar Desh's legacy. But they consume news in seconds on mobile—via instant breaking alerts and audio briefs. Without an app, you lose this entire generation to unverified social media rumors.
4. **Serving the Patriotic Global Diaspora:**  
   Millions of Bangladeshis in the UK, USA, Canada, and the Middle East want to read Amar Desh every morning. Physical print copies cannot reach London or New York; a mobile app delivers the morning ePaper replica instantly.
5. **Eliminating Fake Scam Apps:**  
   Because there is no official app, unauthorized clone apps on Google Play are misusing the Amar Desh masthead and profiting off your name. An official verified app reclaims your brand.
