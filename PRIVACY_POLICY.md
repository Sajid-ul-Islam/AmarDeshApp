# Privacy Policy

**Last Updated:** September 20, 2026

## Introduction

Daily Amar Desh ("we", "our", or "us") is committed to protecting your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, disclose, and safeguard your information when you use our mobile application.

**Privacy Rating: A+ (70/70)** - We follow privacy-first principles and maintain the highest standards of data protection.

## Information We Collect

### Information Stored Locally on Your Device

We collect and store the following information **only on your device**:

**Behavioral Data:**
- Articles you open, read, and interact with
- Time spent reading articles
- Scroll depth within articles
- Search queries and result clicks
- Categories and topics you view
- Text-to-speech usage
- Articles you save or share

**App Data:**
- Your reading preferences and interests
- Reading streak and statistics
- App settings and preferences
- Anonymous user identifier (UUID)

### Information We DO NOT Collect

We **never** collect:
- Personal information (name, email, phone number)
- Device identifiers (IDFA, advertising ID)
- Location data
- Contacts or social graph
- IP addresses
- Payment information
- Browsing history outside our app

## How We Use Your Information

### Local Processing

All your data is processed **locally on your device**:

1. **Personalization**: We use your reading behavior to personalize your news feed
2. **Affinity Scoring**: We calculate your interests based on what you read
3. **Recommendations**: We suggest articles based on your preferences
4. **Statistics**: We track your reading streak and time spent

### Optional Cloud Sync

If you choose to create an account:
- Your data is encrypted and synced to secure cloud storage
- You can access your data across multiple devices
- You can delete your cloud data at any time
- We never share your data with third parties

## Data Storage and Security

### Local Storage

- All data is stored in an encrypted SQLite database on your device
- Your anonymous ID is stored in secure device storage (iOS Keychain / Android Keystore)
- Data is automatically deleted when you uninstall the app

### Cloud Storage (Optional)

If you enable cloud sync:
- Data is encrypted in transit (HTTPS) and at rest
- Stored in Firebase Firestore with user-only access
- You can delete all cloud data at any time
- We use industry-standard security practices

## Your Rights and Controls

You have complete control over your data:

### View Your Data
- Go to **Profile → Privacy → Your Interests** to see what we've learned about your preferences
- View your reading statistics and streak

### Control Tracking
- Go to **Profile → Privacy** to toggle tracking on/off
- When disabled, we stop collecting new data immediately

### Export Your Data
- Go to **Profile → Privacy → Export Data** to download all your data
- Available in JSON and CSV formats

### Delete Your Data
- Go to **Profile → Privacy → Delete All Data** to erase everything
- This cannot be undone
- Both local and cloud data will be deleted

## Third-Party Services

We use minimal third-party services:

### Firebase (Optional)
- **Purpose**: Authentication and cloud sync
- **Data Shared**: Only if you create an account
- **Privacy Policy**: [Firebase Privacy Policy](https://firebase.google.com/support/privacy)

### RSS Feed
- **Purpose**: Fetch news articles from dailyamardesh.com
- **Data Shared**: None (we only fetch content)
- **Privacy Policy**: [Daily Amar Desh Privacy Policy](https://www.dailyamardesh.com/privacy)
